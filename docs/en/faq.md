# ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## 📊 Data Management

### **Can I bulk import data from Excel or CSV?**

**Yes.** Go to the **Emissions** page and click the "Import" button. We support standard CSV templates. You can download a sample template from that screen.

### **How is the Carbon Tax calculated?**

The tax is an estimation based on:

1. Your total embedded emissions (Scope 1 + Scope 2 + Scope 3).
2. Minus any free allowances you might have (under ETS).
3. Multiplied by the current weekly average price of EU ETS Carbon permits.

### **Can I manage multiple facilities?**

**Yes.** You can define multiple facilities (factories, warehouses) under the **Settings > Organization** menu. Emissions can be tagged per facility.

## 📄 Reporting

### **What formats are supported for export?**

* **PDF:** Best for internal sharing and management presentations.
* **XML:** Compliant with the official EU CBAM Transitional Registry structure.
* **Excel:** For raw data analysis.

### **Can I edit a report after generating it?**

Once a report is marked as "Submitted", it becomes read-only to ensure audit trails. However, you can create a "Correction Report" referenced to the original period.

## 🔐 Security & Access

### **I forgot my password. How do I reset it?**

Click the "Forgot Password?" link on the login screen. You will receive an email with a secure link to reset your credentials.

### **Can I give my auditor view-only access?**

**Yes.** Create a new user invite for them and assign the **Viewer** role. They will be able to see dashboards and reports but cannot edit data.
