# 📖 User Manual & Guide

Welcome to the **CBAM Guard User Manual**. This guide will help you navigate the platform, manage emissions, and generate compliance reports.

## 🎛️ The Dashboard

The Dashboard is your command center. When you log in, you are greeted with a real-time overview of your sustainability metrics.

### Key Metrics (KPIs)

1. **Total Emissions (tCO2e):** Your aggregated Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions for the current year.
2. **Estimated Tax:** The projected financial liability based on current CBAM regulations and ETS prices.
3. **Active Alerts:** Critical notifications requiring immediate attention (e.g., quota exceedance).

## 🏭 Managing Emissions

To add or update emission data:

1. Navigate to the **Emissions** tab in the sidebar.
2. Click **"Add Entry"** (top right).
3. **Fill in the form:**
    * **Source Type:** E.g., Natural Gas (Scope 1) or Electricity (Scope 2).
    * **Facility:** Select the factory or unit.
    * **Amount:** Enter the consumption value (e.g., kWh or m3).
4. The system automatically calculates the **Carbon Equivalent (tCO2e)** using the latest emission factors.

> **Tip:** You can also bulk upload data using the CSV Import tool.

## 📄 Generating Reports

Compliance relies on accurate reporting. CBAM Guard simplifies this into a few clicks.

### Types of Reports

* **Quarterly CBAM Declaration:** The official format required by the EU commission.
* **Internal Audit:** A detailed breakdown for internal stakeholders.
* **Supplier Summary:** Aggregated data from your supply chain.

### How to Export

1. Go to the **Reports** page.
2. Select the **Date Range** (e.g., Q1 2024).
3. Choose the **Format** (PDF for reading, XML for official submission).
4. Click **Generate**. The file will download automatically.

## 🧠 AI Assistant

The **CBAM AI Assistant** acts as your regulatory expert sitting right in the app.

**What can you ask?**

* *"What is the emission factor for steel production in Turkey?"*
* *"How does the new Oct 2023 regulation affect my aluminum exports?"*
* *"Analyze my Scope 1 trend and suggest reductions."*

**Context Awareness:**
If you are viewing a specific chart (e.g., Emission Trend), the AI is aware of *that specific data* and can provide context-aware insights.

## 🌍 Supplier Portal

You can invite suppliers to input their own data directly, ensuring accuracy for Scope 3 emissions.

1. Go to **Suppliers**.
2. Click **"Invite Supplier"**.
3. Enter their email. They will receive a secure **Magic Link**.
4. Once they submit data, it appears in your dashboard with a "Pending Review" status.
