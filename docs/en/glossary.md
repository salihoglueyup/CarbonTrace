# 📖 Glossary of Terms

This glossary defines key terms and acronyms used within **CBAM Guard** and the broader EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism regulations.

## C

### **CBAM (Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism)**

A fast-approaching EU regulation that puts a fair price on the carbon emitted during the production of carbon-intensive goods that are entering the EU, and to encourage cleaner industrial production in non-EU countries.

### **CN Code (Combined Nomenclature)**

An 8-digit code used in trade dealings to classify goods for customs declarations in the EU. CBAM applies only to specific CN codes (e.g., Cement, Iron & Steel, Aluminum, Fertilizers, Electricity, Hydrogen).

### **Co2e (Carbon Dioxide Equivalent)**

A metric measure used to compare the emissions from various greenhouse gases on the basis of their global-warming potential (GWP), by converting amounts of other gases to the equivalent amount of carbon dioxide with the same global warming potential.

## E

### **Embedded Emissions**

Direct and indirect emissions generated during the production of goods, which are "embedded" in the final product imported into the EU.

### **ETS (Emission Trading System)**

A "cap and trade" system where a limit is set on the total amount of certain greenhouse gases that can be emitted. Companies receive or buy emission allowances, which they can trade with one another as needed.

## S

### **Scope 1 Emissions**

**Direct** emissions from owned or controlled sources.

* *Example:* Fuel combustion in company vehicles, natural gas used in factory furnaces.

### **Scope 2 Emissions**

**Indirect** emissions from the generation of purchased energy.

* *Example:* Electricity, steam, heat, and cooling purchased from a utility provider.

### **Scope 3 Emissions**

All other **indirect** emissions that occur in a company’s value chain.

* *Example:* Business travel, waste disposal, transportation of purchased goods, and emissions from suppliers.

### **Tied Emissions**
