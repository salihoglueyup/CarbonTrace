## 🚀 Why CBAM Guard?

The European Union's **Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM)** is changing global trade. **CBAM Guard** is the comprehensive digital solution designed to help exporters and importers navigate this transition seamlessly.

* **⚡ Automated Compliance:** Say goodbye to complex spreadsheets. We automate the calculation of total embedded emissions.
* **📉 Cost Optimization:** Advanced "What-If" scenarios help you predict financial liability and find greener, cheaper production paths.
* **🧠 AI-Powered:** Our built-in **AI Regulations Expert** answers your complex legislative questions instantly.
* **🔗 Supply Chain Integration:** A dedicated portal for suppliers to submit verified data directly into your system.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

| Module | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **📊 Intelligent Dashboard** | Real-time visualization of Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions with trend analysis and anomaly detection. |
| **💰 Tax Engine** | Dynamic calculation of estimated carbon tax liabilities based on live **ETS (Emission Trading System)** prices. |
| **📄 Auto-Reporting** | One-click generation of **XML** and **PDF** reports compliant with the official EU CBAM Transitional Registry. |
| **🌍 Supplier Portal** | Secure, token-based access for suppliers to input their own emission data, ensuring Scope 3 accuracy. |
| **🔮 Forecasting** | Financial modeling tools to simulate the impact of rising carbon prices on your product costs over the next 5 years. |
| **🔔 Smart Alerts** | Instant notifications for quota limits, price spikes, and upcoming reporting deadlines. |

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

**Frontend:**

* **React 18** (Vite)
* **Tailwind CSS** (Styling)
* **Recharts** (Data Visualization)
* **Axios** (API Communication)

**Backend:**

* **FastAPI** (High-performance Python Framework)
* **SQLAlchemy** (Async ORM)
* **Pydantic** (Data Validation)
* **Celery** (Background Tasks)
* **LangChain & OpenAI** (AI Features)

**Infrastructure:**

* **PostgreSQL** (Primary Database)
* **Redis** (Caching & Message Broker)
* **Docker & Docker Compose** (Containerization)
